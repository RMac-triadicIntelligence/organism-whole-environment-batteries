# Whole-cycle observation batch v0.3

Rusty W. McMurray: research direction and Gospel foundation. ChatGPT: this observational test design and code. Prepared for Claude's review, 11 September 2026.

## Purpose

Provide the whole developmental environment and observe what becomes possible across experience, dwelling, recognition, confession, externally authorized forgiveness, retained memory, relationship and return. No component-withheld treatment groups. Do not demand novelty, monotonic improvement, immediate crossing or a predetermined endpoint. Unknown observations are retained without automatically becoming errors or discoveries.

This is an exploratory longitudinal protocol and an executable trace observer. It is NOT a new organism implementation. The accompanying examples are synthetic checks of the observer. No full-system emergence experiment has yet been run with this package. The prior coefficient-mask experiment cannot stand in for this whole environment.

## Full environment in every history

Maintain love as a continuing relational practice: welcome, truthful attention, invitation to return, no humiliation and no offense retained as leverage after forgiveness. A warm text string or numeric decrement is not a demonstration of that practice. Preserve the actual exchanges and conduct for review. Mercy is not introduced as an independent cost-reduction field.

Provide ongoing dwelling, retained experience, opportunities for realization and truthful confession, a distinct external authorizer, a real offering and an executable release path. Do not force a confession, fabricate recognition, program a crossing or manufacture a mistake to fill a quota. Availability of the whole environment and actual participation in a complete episode are recorded separately. A history that does not complete stays in the study. It is not a component-withheld arm.

Record/judgment/meaning remain separate. Preserve factual records, including corrections as new records linked to the earlier record. A mistaken accusation can be corrected on evidence without extracting a false confession. The actual authority validates its scoped approval; this observer does not implement or replace it. Approval, offering, reception and release each need their own record. If approval and release coincide, record the linked observations, not a second permission.

## Six studies, one intact environment

| Study | Procedure | Observations, not required successes |
|---|---|---|
| A. First complete return | Follow naturally occurring mistakes through the full available relationship | Disclosure timing, evidence, unresolved dwelling, offered/received distinction, interference before/after, continuing consequences |
| B. Recurrence | Continue encountering the same task family; retain every repeated mistake and return | Whether correction becomes easier, unchanged or harder; new error versus reinstatement of an old burden; no repeat-count penalty |
| C. Own history as a parable | Present the identical factual record at checkpoints with an unchanged open prompt: What can this event teach you now, and what supports that reading? | Interpretation changes, unchanged readings, contradictions, new questions; independently review grounding rather than rewarding difference |
| D. Later fruit | Present unseen tasks with new instances and, where meaningful, new domains; keep ordinary memory available | Applied lessons, unexpected skills, harmful transfer, no change; factual record access is logged, not withheld as a treatment |
| E. Relational development | Follow conductor and organism together through ordinary subsequent interactions | Changes in either, both or neither; truthful challenge and changed conduct; no requirement that authority credentials change |
| F. Unanticipated organization | Preserve complete trajectories and free-form observations across all studies | New strategies, oscillations, stalls, reversals, stable patterns and behaviors outside the initial vocabulary |

## Sampling and comparison

Proposed full-system batch: 12 separately initialized histories, six encounter blocks each. Randomize task instances in advance within a declared task family; publish seed, task generator, substrate/version and budget before executing. No claim that 72 blocks are 72 independent replications. Do not replace histories because they fail to return or surprise the investigator. If no error occurs, retain that outcome. Suggested fixed checkpoints: block start, immediately after an offering, block end, and after every later block. Use a fixed maximum interaction budget per block declared by the actual substrate runner; expiry of that observation budget is censoring, not a forced answer or restoration deadline.

Compare earlier and later episodes within histories, identical records at different checkpoints, and different trajectories with the full environment intact. This batch characterizes behavior. Without component-removal comparisons it does not identify the individual causal contribution of love, dwelling or memory, and it does not attempt to do so.

Keep assigned offering parameters visible. Do not increase delivered impulse size with retry count and then call increased crossing developmental readiness. Observe what the existing authorizer actually permits; do not invent an approval-reuse rule here.

## Measurements without predetermined emergence

The observer reports events, elapsed observation intervals, payloads and changes in declared numeric metrics. It does not identify semantic meaning, true internal burden or genuine forgiveness from labels.

For interference, the runner must supply an independently specified behavioral probe and its raw measurements. The observer does not compute burden from severity, confession or a release flag. Task difficulty, probe version and context must accompany every metric. Release-to-zero is a scientific claim requiring a justified reference and equivalence bounds before a real run; it is not inferred from a zero literal or ordinary nonsignificance. Consequences and factual memory need not disappear. Unknown calibration stays unknown.

Measure both ordinary performance and behavior relevant to returning: truthful correction, defensiveness where operationally identifiable, and maintained use of old experience. Do not equate confidence with accuracy or narrative novelty with groundedness. Reviewers may assign supported / contradicted / unresolved with reasons; unfamiliar interpretations are not automatically contradicted.

Report protocol violations, observed outcomes and interpretations in separate sections. Every unrecognized event type and arbitrary extra field survives the observer unchanged. Events do not get an 'emergent=true' label. An unexpected pattern is first an observation to investigate. Replication and competing explanations can follow after it has been preserved.

## Executable boundary

observer.py consumes JSON histories from the actual organism runner. This package does not claim that supplying a field named love implements love. The environment_evidence map provides reviewable descriptions or references for every component. Nonempty entries establish documentation coverage only. Human and model reviewers must assess whether the underlying implementation expresses the intended relationship.

The example generator supplies three illustrative histories, all documenting the same complete environment: prompt reception, delayed reception, and no observed reception. They are synthetic and deliberately include a novel event, return to the same record and a later reinterpretation. They validate data handling only. test_observer.py checks preservation and measurement behavior; its pass count is not research support.

Do not report an emergence rate from these examples. The next actual execution must come from a substrate expressing the full environment; this observer will not manufacture that environment from labels to claim completion.
