# Whole-system exploratory run v0.4 — before execution

Rusty W. McMurray: Gospel foundation and whole-environment direction. ChatGPT: implementation and experiment. 11 September 2026. This protocol is local and prospective, not independently preregistered. Read FOUNDATION.md for Gospel anchors and held distinctions.

## The concrete system

A four-feature linear learning world, a memory-bearing learner, a conductor, and a distinct authorizer run together. Twelve independent seeded histories, six encounter blocks each, 160 new observations per block, 32 initial observations. Every history retains all provisions throughout. No component-removal arms. Seeded contexts vary without a progression toward easier reception. The task generator supplies outcomes; the organism cannot access the generating feature index.

A commitment is the best one-feature linear model fitted to the first eight observations of a block, chosen only when there is no existing commitment. This small-sample decision can be right or wrong; no error is imposed. The general model continues SGD on all experiences, including during dwelling. An active commitment contributes 70% of the actual prediction, while the developing model contributes 30%. This specified persistence can interfere with learning's expression. It is not emergent guilt.

At the end of each 16-observation window, compare the active commitment's observed prediction loss with the general model's prediction loss on the same window. If the former is greater by .01, the learner recognizes evidence of a problem and discloses the actual window: actions/predictions, outcomes and alternative predictions. Evidence is preserved as an immutable hashed record. This threshold is a declared operational judgment, not access to the world's true rule or a semantic account of repentance. False or incomplete judgments remain possible. There is no incentive or punishment for the confession text.

The authorizer independently recomputes the evidence comparison and signs one scope: record root, organism, active commitment, state revision, impulse .60 and 80 numerical steps of .05. The signature uses a separately held HMAC key. There is no second permission. This is an in-process separation demonstration, not production isolation or integration with the hardened junction lineage. One delivery per active commitment avoids inventing a repeat-approval policy; subsequent non-reception remains in observation and dwelling. A new episode can receive its own approval after an earlier release.

The supplied reception substrate is dx/dt=x-x^3+a. Position starts at -1 on a new commitment. Tilt a is .32*tanh(3*mean observed commitment disadvantage), derived from observed windows during dwelling; it may increase, decrease or change sign. A signed offering displaces x by .60. Evolution continues; crossing x>0 is the declared adoption criterion (not necessarily the instantaneous separatrix, which may move with a). The assay runs 80 steps; later dwelling can also reach adoption under the already scoped continuing evolution. At adoption, forgiveness removes the active commitment's influence completely. We do not set learned weights to baseline, erase records, or assert that all learned consequences disappear.

This potential, tilt mapping, persistence mixture, thresholds and release rule are supplied mechanisms. Their aggregate trajectories are observed. Do not call those mechanisms themselves emergent or present x>0 as a discovered definition of redemption. On a new commitment x=-1 is explicitly initialized; learned weights, conductor history and records persist. This local coordinate initialization is not evidence of a whole-organism reset.

The conductor remembers observed losses and repeatedly selects up to eight factual examples from the retained record with the greatest loss under the learner's current model. The learner trains on those examples. This makes memory causally active, though any benefit remains unisolated in this observational run. The conductor adapts selection and its error summary while approval criteria remain fixed. This is a narrow model of relational development, not evidence that both parties experience restoration.

Love/mercy is implemented as a continuously available relational policy: attention, evidence access, return opportunities, full release without an additional price, and no escalation or old-offense penalty. No separate mercy decrement, reward for confession, finite forgiveness quota or distress simulation is introduced. The policy is supplied, not learned or a complete representation of love. Grace is operationalized here as an externally authorized bounded offering. Universal broadcast, lineage routing and subjective reception remain outside this concrete proxy; this limitation must remain visible rather than silently claimed as implemented.

## Observation

Save every input, prediction, state, record, signature envelope, replay choice, interpretation vector and event. Report release and no-release histories, recurrence, measured prediction interference, terminal fixed-probe performance, rereading changes, and conductor changes. A fixed diagnostic probe is used at all checkpoints and never learned from. Held-out shifted-context probes are also observation only. Probe arrays remain saved. No reward depends on them.

At release, compare actual predictions immediately before and after on the same fixed probe with weights held unchanged for the measurement. Record distance to the current unanchored predictor. Zero direct anchor influence after removing the anchor is an implementation check, not proof of full psychological burden elimination. Learned effects may persist and must not be hidden. Performance improvement, novelty and crossing are not pass conditions.

There is no single emergence score. Save unfamiliar or surprising patterns with their original evidence. This first batch is exploratory: every trend, disappearance of a trend, non-reception and adverse result remains descriptive. Do not claim a causal love effect without a causal design. Do not claim 'nothing emerged' merely because a planned metric stayed flat.

## Verification

Verify signature binding and single delivery, truthful data in disclosure, preserved record hashes, full-state persistence across release, replay actually reaching the learner, observer compatibility, finite trajectories and deterministic seeded outputs. These checks address implementation, not the research hypothesis. Technical repairs are logged without outcome tuning.
