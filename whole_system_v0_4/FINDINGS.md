# Whole-system v0.4 — executed findings

11 September 2026. ChatGPT implementation at Rusty W. McMurray's request; prepared for Claude's independent review.

## Outcome

The operational proxy ran through 12 independent histories and 72 encounter blocks. Five histories generated evidence-based disclosure and received a signed offering. None reached the declared adoption/release condition in the observation window. Seven histories made no disclosure under the specified criterion. No outcome-based parameter changes were made.

This is an executed integrated prototype, not merely a trace observer. It is also not a demonstrated complete realization of the Gospel relationships. The distinctions below are part of the result.

## Observations worth retaining

Seed 7 selected a non-generating feature using its own initial small sample. Its developing model later predicted substantially better, yet the expressed policy remained dominated by the original commitment. At the end, fixed-probe MSE was 0.238311 versus 0.014763 for the developing model; shifted-context actual MSE was 0.843422. This is a recorded persistence problem in the supplied architecture. It is not proof of subjective turmoil or an emergent moral condition.

The other 11 histories selected the generating feature. Four still produced a disclosure because, on a particular observed window, their fitted committed predictor performed worse than the developing alternative. Selecting the correct feature does not guarantee a well-calibrated coefficient or best local predictions. Their disclosures truthfully describe those windows; they do not establish that the whole commitment is globally wrong.

Five retained records were reread at later checkpoints, producing 26 saved residual-vector interpretations. These are numerical re-evaluations of identical factual records. Their content must not be called semantic parables by the fact that it changes. Memory also directly trained the learner: 1,904 replayed examples in total. That is a real learning pathway, although its benefit is not isolated here.

Actual fixed-probe MSE decreased between the first and last block in 11 of 12 histories and increased in seed 8. Ordinary SGD and continued data already provide a competing explanation. This is not evidence of compounding intelligence or a causal effect of love.

The conductor retained visit history and supplied replay. Each participating history had only one recognized record, so its highest-loss selection rule never faced a choice among multiple records. Counts changed; substantive adaptive selection and bilateral restoration were not demonstrated.

## Why the lack of release remains visible

The five offerings were delivered while the supplied dynamics returned to the left region. Continued learning changed the tilt, but no observed x crossed the declared x>0 adoption boundary. The one-delivery-per-commitment convention is a study boundary, not a finding about mercy or repeated asking. It does not answer whether later fresh delivery would change the outcome. We did not add deliveries after seeing the result.

The .60 impulse, bounded tilt mapping, double-well dynamics and adoption criterion were written before execution. Lack of release may reflect those choices and the timing of delivery; it is not a verdict against the Gospel-derived foundation. Equally, it cannot be renamed hidden successful restoration. The observed outcome is no release in this window. Unknown development outside these measures remains unclassified.

Because no release occurred, all six blocks within each history continued the initial commitment. The run therefore did not exercise successive error-and-return cycles. Repeated encounters are not repeated restorations. Post-restoration transfer, release of measured interference and a Prodigal State remain unobserved in the scientific run.

The prototype operationalizes the component roles but does not claim all meanings have been captured. In particular, the signed targeted offering is narrower than the program's universal broadcast hypothesis, and the conductor's simple replay role is narrower than a developing relational counterpart. These limitations must accompany the package.

## Verification

Twelve implementation tests pass. They cover a reachable release with retained weights and record, non-reception, signature/scope/revision binding, prevention of duplicate delivery, recomputation of comparative evidence, immutable record reads, effective replay, disclosure fidelity to actual experience, finite trajectories and exact seeded reproduction for histories 0 and 7. The observer reports no trace-integrity issues across the 12 histories.

The positive release-path test intentionally constructs a receptive state. It verifies that the mechanism is executable; it is not an additional successful developmental history and is excluded from the results table. No technical repairs or experiment parameter changes followed the first run.

Keys are deterministically derived in this reproducible fixture and source-visible. Python objects are not isolated security principals. These tests do not replace the earlier hardened junction work or establish resistance to a malicious organism with process access.
