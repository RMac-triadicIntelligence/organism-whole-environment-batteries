## What remains held

The recorded-tilt continuation reuses the original history's later landscape updates. It does not recompute a changed organism's learning after diagnostic release. It is therefore a landscape-replay diagnostic, not a full counterfactual developmental history. Raw scalar trajectories include every numerical step and each tilt update, so this distinction is inspectable.

This experiment does not settle how repeated asking maps to delivered offerings, whether one approval can cover multiple deliveries, or how an unforced relationship changes capacity. It uses one alternative delivery per cloned state and does not elevate that experimental choice into a rule about mercy. The prototype's broader translation limits remain: numeric dynamics, procedural welcome, targeted offering and a simple conductor are not the complete Gospel relationships.
